Conversion by role

A folder counts as reached interview if it has a .git/ repo (Vlad's convention) or appears in interviews.csv. Rates are approximate — small sample sizes per role and the role classifier's folder-name heuristic both add noise. Read as a relative signal, not an absolute one.

Conversion by month

Cohort attribution: an application from month X counts as interviewed if its folder eventually reached interview, regardless of when the interview happened. The most recent month(s) understate — apps sent this month haven't had time to convert yet.
