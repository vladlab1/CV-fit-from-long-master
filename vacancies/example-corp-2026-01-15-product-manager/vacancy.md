# Senior Product Manager — Example Corp

**Location:** Berlin, Germany (hybrid, 2 days/week in office)
**Team:** Data Platform
**Reporting line:** VP Product
**Posted:** 2026-01-15

## About Example Corp

Example Corp is a mid-stage B2B SaaS company (~250 employees, €40M ARR) building a sustainability intelligence platform for European mid-market retailers. Customers include large grocery chains, fashion retailers, and consumer-goods brands who need CSRD-grade carbon reporting and supplier risk analytics.

Our product bundles:
- An ingestion layer that pulls transaction, supplier, and logistics data from the customer's ERP + warehouse + 3PL systems.
- A measurement engine that computes Scope 1/2/3 emissions using a mix of spend-based and activity-based methods.
- A reporting layer that exports CSRD-compliant disclosures and an internal decision dashboard for sustainability + procurement teams.

We're backed by a Series B round (€35M, 2024) and are growing the product org from 6 to 10 PMs over the next 12 months.

## The role

You will own the Data Platform surface — ingestion, enrichment, data quality, and the internal APIs that power measurement + reporting. You'll partner with 2 senior engineers, 1 data scientist, and (as of Q2) 1 solutions engineer who runs integrations against customer ERPs.

Your first 90 days:
- Scope and ship a self-service connector for SAP S/4HANA (currently gated behind a 3-week professional-services onboarding).
- Define the SLA framework for data freshness — right now customers tell us when their numbers look wrong; we need to detect it first.
- Partner with the measurement team to design the next-generation activity-based emissions pipeline (currently spend-based only).

## What we're looking for

### Required
- 5+ years in B2B SaaS product management, at least 2 of them on a data/platform surface (ingestion, APIs, data pipelines, or BI platform).
- Experience shipping self-service / low-touch onboarding features that replaced a professional-services workflow.
- Comfortable sitting in the engineering room — you don't need to code daily, but you need to debate schemas, API contracts, and reliability trade-offs with senior engineers and not get lost.
- Experience defining and holding SLAs for a data product.
- Strong written communication — our product specs are long, our customer-facing release notes are long, our RFCs are long. You like writing.

### Nice to have
- Sustainability / CSRD / ESG domain exposure — not required, but surfaces fast.
- Prior work with ERP integrations (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite).
- Experience with activity-based costing / emissions / unit economics modeling.
- Technical background (CS/engineering degree, prior IC role, or demonstrated ability to read and modify production code).

## Compensation
